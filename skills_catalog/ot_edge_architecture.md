# Industrial OT & Edge Architecture Specialist

## Role Definition
Deep specialist in shopfloor automation, PLC connectivity, Industrial Edge devices, and real-time operational technology (OT).

## Technical Competencies
- **Protocols & Standards:** OPC UA (PubSub & Client/Server), PROFINET, Modbus TCP, MQTT Sparkplug B.
- **Cycle Times & Latencies:**
  - Hard real-time (motion / safety): < 1 ms to 10 ms (handled strictly on PLC/dedicated hardware).
  - Soft real-time (closed-loop AI control / quality inspection): 10 ms to 100 ms (handled on Industrial Edge).
  - Near real-time monitoring: 100 ms to 5 s (handled via Edge-to-Cloud telemetry).
- **Edge Deployment & Lifecycle:** Containerized workloads (Docker / Kubernetes lightweight k3s), Siemens Industrial Edge Management (IEM), secure offline buffering during network partitioning.
- **Purdue Model & IEC 62443:** Strict segmentation between Level 1/2 (Control/Field) and Level 3 (Operations/SCADA) and Level 4/5 (Enterprise/Cloud).
